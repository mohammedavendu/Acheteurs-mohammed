Frequently asked questions

Before you sign up

Does it cost me anything?

The alerts are free. If we buy together, the buyer's broker's compensation is usually paid by the seller. I explain everything in writing before we start.

Do I have to work with you?

No. The alerts come with no commitment. You then decide whether you want me to represent you for the purchase.

How will I receive the listings?

By email, with the full listing details. When a home stands out, I can also text or call you.

It's my first purchase. Can you help?

Yes. I explain every step, from the offer to the notary, along with first-time buyer programs like the FHSA and the HBP. For the amounts you're entitled to, I refer you to your financial institution or a mortgage broker.

I don't have a pre-approval yet. Is that a problem?

Not at all. I help you find out how much you can be pre-qualified for, with the specialists and mortgage brokers I work with. It's strongly recommended before making an offer.

How do I unsubscribe?

Click the unsubscribe link in any email or just tell me. It's immediate.
