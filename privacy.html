Information we collect

  • Buyer alerts: property type, areas, budget, purchase timeline, financing status, first name, cell phone number, email address and your consent.
  • Seller market analysis: property type, property address and city, selling timeline, purchase plans, first name, cell phone number, email address and your consent.
  • First home buyer guide: first name, email address and your consent.
  • Calculator: calculations happen in your browser and nothing is sent. If you then fill out the alerts form, the estimated amount is attached to your request.
  • Technical information attached to your request: the page visited, the ad source (for example a Google or Facebook ad) and the headline version shown.
  • On your device only: your quiz answers are kept in your browser so you can pick up where you left off. They are erased once the form is sent.

Why we use it

  • To send you listings that match your criteria, or to prepare the comparative market analysis of your property.
  • To contact you by email, text or phone about your project, according to the consent given.
  • To put you in touch with a mortgage broker for your pre-qualification, only if you ask.
  • To measure how well our ads perform.
  • We do not use your information for other purposes without your consent, and we never sell it.

Who it is shared with

  • Web3Forms, which sends your forms by email, and Google (Gmail), where they are received.
  • Vercel, which hosts the site.
  • Google Ads and Meta (Facebook, Instagram), to measure ad conversions, when these tools are enabled. Your email and phone number may be sent to them in encrypted (hashed) form to match a request to an ad.
  • A mortgage broker or other professional, only with your agreement.
  • Some of these providers store data outside Quebec, including in the United States. We choose providers that offer recognized safeguards.

Cookies and measurement tools

  • When enabled, Google Ads and Meta tools may use cookies to measure visits and requests coming from our ads.
  • These cookies are off by default. They are turned on only if you accept them in the banner shown on your first visit. You can change your mind at any time with the "Cookie preferences" link at the bottom of the pages.
  • You can refuse or delete cookies in your browser settings, and manage your ad preferences in your Google and Meta accounts.

Retention and security

  • We keep your information as long as needed for the purposes above, then destroy it securely, unless the law or OACIQ rules require us to keep it longer.
  • The site uses an encrypted connection (HTTPS), and access to your information is limited to those who need it.

Your rights

  • You may at any time ask to access your information, have it corrected, withdraw your consent or unsubscribe. Write to Mohammedavendu@gmail.com or to Vendirect inc. at the contact details above. We reply within 30 days.
  • You may also file a complaint with the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec (cai.gouv.qc.ca).
  • This site is not intended for people under 14.

Changes

  • We may update this policy. The date at the top of the page shows the latest version.
